This site reflects my journey through the hobby: learning, building, testing equipment, operating portable and exploring how radio fits into today's technological landscape.

Here you will find material about:

- **Amateur radio operation** and portable setups.
- **Propagation, bands and operating techniques**.
- **Antennas, RF experiments and station improvements**.
- **Software, SDR, and radio-related development projects**.
- **Infrastructure ideas for modern ham radio**.
- **Ideas at the intersection of radio, networking and distributed systems**.

Many of the things documented here come from real experiments in the field, portable activations or late-night technical curiosity sessions. Think of this site as a living lab notebook for radio, part technical documentation, part exploration log. This is not meant to be a static reference manual but rather a **living notebook** of things I learn, build, test or experiment with along the way.
