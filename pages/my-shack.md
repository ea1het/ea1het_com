<p>In the past I used to operate QRO only from my home. Some time ago, after careful consideration on the roots of why I decided to enter the ham radio community, I came to the conclusion that my operational mode was simply wrong. Then I decided to shift portable and QRP seeking maximum antenna efficiency and better energy management.</p>

<p>This is how I do radio now:</p>
