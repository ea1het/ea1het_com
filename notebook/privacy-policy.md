## Who we are

This is the personal website of Jonathan González, amateur radio operator EA1HET, located in Spain. Website: [http://ea1het.com](https://ea1het.com).

## Data I do collect

None.

This site does not use analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, contact forms, user accounts or comment systems. No personal data is collected or stored by this site.

## Third-party embedded content

Some pages display live data from external services, including ClubLog, RBN (rbn.telegraphy.de), prop.kc2g.com, NOAA SWPC and OutdoorDX.

When your browser loads this content, those services may log your IP address or set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies. This site has no control over that.

## Hosting infrastructure

Web server access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) are generated automatically by the hosting provider. These are not accessed or used by this site for any purpose.
