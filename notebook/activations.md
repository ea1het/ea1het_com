# Activations

> **Last update:** 2026-05-24

Portable field operations from parks, summits, islands and lighthouses. All operations are QRP (≤ 5 W) unless noted.

All QSOs are uploaded to ClubLog and confirmed via LoTW. Direct QSL available upon request. Please [contact me](/notebook/contact).



## 2024

### Field deployment — July 2024

JPC-12 (PAC-12) portable vertical deployed for the first time with a custom counterpoise plate modification. Bands tested: 10 m through 40 m. Full build details in the [Tuning the JPC-12](/notebook/articles/turning-jpc-12) article.
